PDP conversion questions, answered.

+How do I increase my product page conversion rate?
The highest-ROI lever on a product page is the visual content, especially the hero and carousel. Auditing every image against the Visual Conversion Standard (Vizit uses an 80+ score threshold) and fixing the ones below it is the fastest path to lift. Brands that systematically optimize visual content see an average 16.5% conversion lift.
+Why isn't my Amazon listing converting?
Most underperforming Amazon listings lose at the first impression: the hero image doesn't earn the tap, so shoppers scroll past before they ever see the title, price, or reviews. The fix is almost always visual. Vizit identifies which specific visual elements are suppressing your conversion rate and generates the fixes automatically.
+How does pre-syndication scoring fit into our PIM workflow?
Your PIM calls the Vizit API when new images attach to a product record. Vizit returns Hero, Carousel, and PDP scores in seconds, benchmarked against the Content Conversion Standard, with diagnostics on what's limiting conversion. Vizit doesn't replace your PIM or syndication platform — it's a quality gate on top of your stack.
+How long does it take to see results from PDP optimization?
Visual fixes deploy fast. Once an updated image is live, every new shopper sees the optimized version immediately. Most Vizit customers see measurable lift within days of deploying Vizit Certified image updates across their priority SKUs.
