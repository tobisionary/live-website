Carousel questions, answered.

+What is the Vizit Carousel Score?
A 0–100 rating of how effectively a product's secondary images carry a shopper from interest to purchase, benchmarked against the top performers in its category. It accounts for each image's individual conversion power, whether the carousel covers the content shoppers in the category expect, and the order the images appear in.
+How many carousel images should an Amazon listing have?
Amazon allows up to seven images visible on the listing. More images aren't automatically better — the carousel that converts covers the questions shoppers in your category ask, in the order they ask them, with no redundant images. Vizit identifies which images are missing, which are redundant, and which are underperforming.
+Does Vizit re-order the carousel automatically?
Yes. Vizit monitors each image's score and re-orders the carousel so the highest-converting images come first, re-merchandising as performance shifts. Teams can also review and approve recommendations before they go live.
