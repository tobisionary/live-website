Retail media questions, answered.

+Why is my retail media ROAS low?
Most retail media underperformance is caused by the destination PDP, not the bid or the targeting. The hero image determines whether shoppers tap. The carousel determines whether they convert. If either fails, every click underperforms regardless of how precisely the audience is targeted.
+How do I lower my Amazon ACoS?
ACoS is a function of conversion rate and CPC, and both are driven by visual content. A weak hero lowers CTR, which raises CPC. A weak PDP lowers conversion, which raises ACoS further. Brands that bring their PDPs to a Vizit Score of 80 or above typically see ACoS drop within weeks of the update.
+How do I lower my Amazon CPC?
Amazon CPC is influenced by relevance, which is influenced by CTR, which is driven primarily by the hero image. Raising a hero's Vizit Score improves CTR, which improves relevance, which lowers effective CPC.
+Does image quality affect Amazon sponsored ads?
Yes. Every sponsored placement is won or lost at the hero image. Image quality affects CTR, which affects relevance, CPC, and placement. A low Vizit Score on the hero directly suppresses campaign performance, even with perfect targeting and bidding.
