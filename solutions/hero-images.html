Hero image questions, answered.

+What is an Amazon hero image?
An Amazon hero image (also called the main product image) is the primary photograph displayed for a product across Amazon search results, sponsored placements, and product detail pages. It determines whether a shopper clicks the product, and it appears identically across mobile, desktop, and AI-driven discovery surfaces. Amazon requires a pure white background, no text or graphic overlays, and a minimum resolution of 1000×1000 pixels.
+What makes a good Amazon hero image?
A high-performing hero image communicates the brand, product, and variant instantly at thumbnail scale; speaks the visual language shoppers in its category respond to; stands out against the competing products around it; and meets Amazon's technical requirements. Vizit's Hero Image Score measures all four on a 0–100 scale, calibrated to the live competitive context of each category.
+How do I increase glance views on Amazon?
Glance views are driven by what shoppers see in search results, not the product page itself. The hero image decides whether a shopper clicks through. Optimizing it for thumbnail clarity, mobile readability, and the visual triggers proven to convert in your category is the highest-leverage way to increase glance views, CTR, and sponsored ad ROAS — because every paid placement uses the same hero image.
+What is thumb-stopping content?
Thumb-stopping content is visual content engineered to interrupt a shopper's scroll on mobile — commanding attention in the fraction of a second before the thumb keeps moving. Vizit's Hero Image Score measures thumb-stopping performance with shoppers in each specific category.
