Catalog optimization questions, answered.

+How does Vizit score an entire catalog?
Vizit ingests every product page in your catalog, from Amazon or any retailer, and scores each one against the Content Conversion Standard for its category. A typical catalog of 10,000+ SKUs is scored in hours, with every product ranked by its estimated revenue impact.
+How does Vizit estimate revenue impact?
Each product's Product Page Score is combined with its traffic and revenue to estimate how much more it would sell at the standard. A low score on a high-traffic product ranks far above a low score on a product few shoppers visit, so the list reflects what the fix is actually worth.
+Do we have to fix everything Vizit flags?
No. Most of the upside sits in a small share of products. Start at the top of the ranked list and work down as far as your team's capacity allows; the estimate on each row tells you when the return stops justifying the effort.
