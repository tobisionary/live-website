Frequently asked

Perceptual AI, explained.

+What is Perceptual AI?
Perceptual AI is a class of artificial intelligence trained to predict how a specific human audience will respond to visual content at the moment of first perception. Unlike computer vision (which identifies image contents) or generative AI (which creates images), Perceptual AI models the behavioral response: whether a shopper will choose a product based on what they see.
+How is Perceptual AI different from computer vision?
Computer vision answers "what is in this image?" Perceptual AI answers "how will this audience respond to this image?" The training objective is behavioral, grounded in real shopping behavior. Computer vision operates before the decision moment; Perceptual AI predicts the decision itself.
+Who invented Perceptual AI?
Perceptual AI was invented by Jehan Hamedi, founder of Vizit. Vizit holds 45+ patents in the field, and was named Computer Vision Company of the Year by the AI Breakthrough Awards out of 5,000 global nominations for its pioneering work in Perceptual AI and visual intelligence.
+What is Perceptual AI used for?
Primarily ecommerce conversion optimization: scoring product images for predicted purchase likelihood. The underlying capability also applies to retail media, advertising, AI shopping agents, packaging design, and any context where visual content influences behavioral outcomes.
