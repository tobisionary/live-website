The Vizit Score, explained.

+What is the Vizit Score?
The Vizit Score is a 0–100 predictive rating of a product image's or PDP's conversion power, benchmarked against its category. An 80+ score ranks an asset in the top 20% of its category for conversion likelihood — that's the Vizit Certified threshold, the standard the world's best commerce teams brief to, approve against, and syndicate by.
+What is a good Vizit Score?
80 or above. At 80+, an asset is predicted to win the first-impression moment against the majority of competing products in its category. Brands that move low-scoring PDPs on Vizit to an 80+ score see an average 16.5% conversion lift.
+Is the Vizit Score the same for every shopper audience?
No. Vizit Scores are audience- and category-specific. The same image scores differently depending on who's looking at it and what it's competing against. This is a core patent held by Vizit.
+How much does conversion improve with a higher Vizit Score?
Conversion lift scales with the Vizit Score. On average, every 10-point increase unlocks up to a 4% relative conversion rate improvement.
